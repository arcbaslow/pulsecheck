Pulsecheck lives in DevTools

Recording runs in the DevTools panel, not in this popup.

  1. Open a normal website
  2. Press F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I; on macOS ⌘⌥I
  3. Select the Pulsecheck tab next to Elements and Network, or under » if the strip is crowded

If DevTools was already open when you installed the extension, close it and open it again. The panel never appears on chrome:// pages; that is a Chrome restriction.

Sessions stay in memory and nothing is sent off-device. Privacy policy
